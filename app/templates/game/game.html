Durak Rules
Objective

The objective of Durak is to get rid of all your cards. The last player with cards is the "durak" (fool).

Card Rankings

Cards are ranked from highest to lowest: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6

Trump Suit

One suit is randomly selected as the trump suit. Trump cards beat any card of another suit.

Gameplay
  1. The player with the lowest trump card attacks first.
  2. The attacker plays a card, and the defender must beat it with a higher card of the same suit or any trump card.
  3. The attacker can add more cards with the same rank as cards already on the table.
  4. If the defender successfully defends against all cards, the cards are discarded and the defender becomes the next attacker.
  5. If the defender cannot or chooses not to defend, they must take all the cards on the table, and the attacker gets another turn.
