Durak Game Rules

Game Overview

Durak (which means "fool" in Russian) is a traditional Russian card game played with a deck of 36 cards (from 6 to Ace). The objective is to get rid of all your cards. The player left holding cards at the end becomes the "durak" (the fool).

Card Rankings

Cards are ranked from lowest to highest as follows: 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace.

The trump suit is determined at the beginning of the game by randomly selecting a card from the deck. Cards of the trump suit outrank any card of a non-trump suit.

Basic Rules

Dealing

Each player is dealt 6 cards. The next card is turned face up to determine the trump suit and is placed under the deck.

First Move

The player with the lowest trump card goes first. If no player has a trump, the player with the lowest card starts the game.

Attacking

The attacker can play any card from their hand. If there are already cards on the table, the attacker may play additional cards that match the rank of any card already in play.

Defending

To defend, a player must beat each attacking card with a higher card of the same suit or with any trump card. If the attacking card is a trump, only a higher trump card can beat it.

Round End

If the defender beats all attacking cards, they become the attacker for the next round. If they fail, they must pick up all cards on the table, and the attacker remains the same.

Replenishing

After each round, players draw cards from the deck to restore their hand to 6 cards, with the attacker drawing first.

Advanced Rules

Multiple Attackers

Other players can add cards to the attack if they have cards of the same rank as any card already on the table.

Attack Limits

The total number of attacking cards cannot exceed the number of cards in the defender's hand.

Special Rules

Passing

If the defender successfully beats all cards, they may have the option to "pass" the attack to the next player.

Adding Cards

In some variants, the attacker can continue adding cards as long as they match the rank of any card already on the table.

End of Game

Once the deck is empty, players continue playing with the cards in their hands. The game ends when one player discards all their cards. The last player holding cards is declared the "durak" (the loser).
